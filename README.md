EcoWatt is an intelligent household energy forecasting and optimization platform designed to help users better understand, predict, and manage their electricity consumption. The platform combines artificial intelligence, data analytics, weather intelligence, and real-world 
energy grid data to provide actionable insights that reduce electricity costs and minimize environmental impact.

1. The Problem

Many households use electricity without knowing when energy prices are highest, when renewable energy availability is greatest, or how daily consumption habits affect monthly utility bills and carbon emissions. As a result, appliances are often operated during peak-demand periods, leading to higher expenses and increased pressure on the electrical grid.

2. The Solution

EcoWatt addresses this challenge by collecting household information, appliance usage patterns, historical consumption records, weather forecasts, and energy market data. Advanced machine learning models analyze past behavior and accurately predict future electricity consumption for upcoming days and weeks.

3. Personalized Energy Profiles

Users create detailed household profiles including resident information, appliance inventory, and usage frequency. The platform learns individual consumption patterns and combines them with weather forecasts and energy grid statistics to generate personalized energy insights and forecasts.

4. AI Forecasting Engine

The forecasting engine utilizes machine learning models such as Prophet and Scikit-learn to identify trends, seasonal variations, and usage behaviors. Predictions are generated using multiple factors including time, temperature, weather conditions, renewable energy generation levels, holidays, and historical electricity consumption data.

5. Smart Energy Optimization

Beyond forecasting, EcoWatt provides intelligent recommendations for operating energy-intensive appliances at optimal times. By analyzing electricity pricing trends and renewable energy availability, the platform identifies cost-effective and environmentally friendly operating schedules.

6. Interactive Analytics Dashboard

Users gain access to a powerful analytics dashboard featuring interactive charts and visualizations. The dashboard enables monitoring of energy consumption trends, comparison of forecasted versus actual usage, tracking of estimated electricity expenses, and evaluation of carbon footprint over time.

7. What-If Energy Simulation

EcoWatt includes a simulation module that allows users to experiment with different appliance schedules and instantly view their impact on projected energy costs, electricity consumption, and carbon emissions before implementing changes.

8. Modern Full-Stack Architecture

The platform is built using a modern full-stack architecture with Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, and React Query on the frontend. Data management is handled through PostgreSQL and Supabase, while Python-based machine learning services power forecasting and optimization features.

9. Sustainability Impact

EcoWatt promotes sustainable energy consumption by helping households shift demand away from peak periods, increase awareness of energy usage patterns, and reduce carbon emissions. The platform supports the transition toward a cleaner, more efficient, and environmentally responsible energy ecosystem.
